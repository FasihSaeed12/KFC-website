ABOUT KFC

A TASTE THAT INSPIRES

KFC, also known as Kentucky Fried Chicken, is one of the world's best-known fried chicken restaurant brands.

Founded by Colonel Harland Sanders, KFC became famous for its signature fried chicken and its iconic blend of 11 herbs and spices.

From a small beginning to a global restaurant brand, the KFC story is about passion, flavor and a love of great food.
